# Miss Direction

Daily arrow memory game by smAisle. Colored arrows flash, you fill the row in order. 10 rounds a day, 3 wrong answers and you are done, first play locks the score. Files: index.html, owner.html (reads md_devices), manifest.json, icon-180, icon-512, icon-1024.

## v1.11 (Nov 5)
Color boxes now run up a side bar on the left by default. Settings has Color boxes: Side bar or Below. A quick tap right after a swipe now counts.

## v1.12 (Nov 5)
Side bar color boxes grow to fill the full height, so 2, 3 or 4 colors always fill the bar.

## v1.13 (Nov 5)
Color boxes can sit on the left, right or below (Settings). New players are asked once, on first Play, which hand they swipe with: Right puts the boxes on the left, Left puts them on the right. Existing players stay on the left.

## v1.14 (Nov 5)
Keep playing: after 3 wrong, or after quitting, a player picks up at the round they stopped on with a fresh 3 wrong. The first score stays locked. After clearing round 10, Replay starts over at round 1. Progress is on the device under md-at-(date), and it works for Archive days too.

## v1.15 (Nov 6)
From Nov 7, rounds with 2 directions can use any two directions, not just an opposite pair, so round 1 has 12 versions instead of 4. Earlier days and the Archive keep their arrows.
